description=[h1]✒️ Project Writing[/h1]
description=Writing in Project Zomboid is a grey text box. This mod turns it into paper: the real page of the notebook you are holding, the game's own handwriting on its lines, the pen you actually carry. And it does the same for maps. 🧡
description=
description=[h2]📔 On paper[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] ✍️ Every writable item in the game, each on its own kind of paper
description=[*] 📖 Two-page notebooks and journals, with no text across the fold
description=[*] 🖋️ The game's handwritten font, sitting on the printed lines
description=[*] 📄 More pages: 20 for notepads, 30 for notebooks, 80 for journals
description=[*] 💾 Saved as you write, reopened on the last page you wrote
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🗺️ On maps[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] ✏️ Freehand drawing, notes and symbols, on the world map and on paper maps
description=[*] ✋ Move, rotate, resize and erase anything — [b]Ctrl+Z[/b] undoes
description=[*] 📐 Notes and symbols can lie flat on the isometric map
description=[*] 🌙 Night mode, by hand or following the world's clock
description=[*] ✂️ Cut out a piece of the world map and hand it over as a real map
description=[*] 📍 141 extra symbols, sorted by category
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🖌️ Your pens[/h2]
description=The toolbar shows the tools you really carry — no pen, no writing. Every ink keeps its colour, the pencil writes with its tip and erases with its rubber, and pens from other mods simply work.
description=
description=[h2]🤝 Sharing[/h2]
description=In multiplayer, choose once who sees what you draw — only you, your faction, your safehouse, chosen players or everyone — then just draw. Server permissions apply, and sharing can be turned off.
description=
description=[h2]📖 Reading[/h2]
description=Flyers, newspapers and printed maps open in a Neat panel, with the text transcription one click away.
description=
description=[h2]⚙️ Settings[/h2]
description=[b]Options → Mods → Project Writing[/b]: pause while writing, no pause on the world map, vanilla map, sharing, symbol packs.
description=[b]Sandbox[/b]: turn freehand drawing off, or give every player the vanilla map.
description=Drag the panel's corner to resize it.
description=
